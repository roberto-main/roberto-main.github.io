The Farmer Was Replaced

The game "The Farmer Was Replaced" is about coding and giving instructions to a drone on the actions to execute on a soil.
The player is given a plot of land where they can plant different types of crops.
Once harvested, these crops provide the user with resources that can be used to purchase upgrades or unlock the ability to grow new types of crops.
